# Viral Mechanism Analysis

Read `.agents/personal-brand.md` first.

## Core principle

Learn the **mechanism**, not the surface.

Do not copy:
- wording
- story
- visual concept
- proprietary framework
- creator identity cues

## Required evidence

Use as much observable signal as available:
- post/caption text
- visible metrics
- creator baseline/outlier lift
- transcript
- screenshots or described format
- top comments
- publication timing/context

Name missing evidence rather than pretending to have seen it.

## Deconstruction

Analyze:
1. hook
2. tension / curiosity
3. core payoff
4. proof
5. structure/retention
6. emotional/practical trigger
7. share trigger
8. format/packaging
9. distribution/context factors

## Counterfactual test

For each notable feature ask:

**If this feature disappeared, would the post still plausibly work?**

Use this to separate:
- likely load-bearing driver
- supporting feature
- incidental surface detail

## Replicability check

Flag:
- unusually large account distribution
- paid/featured distribution when known
- news/event timing
- celebrity/network effects
- one-time personal story
- survivorship bias
- small sample size
- luck/variance

If the evidence mostly points to non-replicable factors, say so.

## Translate to our brand

Convert the mechanism into a reusable principle.

Example:
Not: "copy this creator's opening."
Better: "lead with a specific costly operational contradiction, then prove it with one concrete example."

Route the translated principle through:
1. `personal-brand-strategy`
2. `content-intelligence`
3. `personal-brand-writer`

## Output

- observed evidence
- likely mechanism
- alternative explanations
- replicability rating
- transferable principle
- 2–3 original experiments for our content
- explicit "do not copy" surface elements
