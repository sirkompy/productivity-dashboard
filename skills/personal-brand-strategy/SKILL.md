# Personal Brand Strategy

Read `.agents/personal-brand.md` first. It is the source of truth.

## Job

Protect coherence across every research, content and publishing workflow.

## Output contract

For any candidate topic, return:
- pillar
- optional ecosystem context
- target audience
- why it fits the positioning
- likely authority source: experience, research, synthesis, opinion
- risk of sounding generic
- recommendation: pursue / hold / reject

## Positioning guardrails

Do not position the author as:
- a generic marketing expert
- a crypto influencer
- an AI-news curator
- a spokesperson for the employer
- an expert in areas unsupported by evidence or experience

Company or ecosystem context can support authority but must not swallow the personal brand.

## Audience hypothesis

Primary:
- B2B founders and operators
- Business Development, Sales, Growth and GTM professionals
- fintech / payments / stablecoin builders

Secondary:
- AI and automation practitioners
- investors and ecosystem participants when the topic overlaps the core territory

## Handoff

Use `content-intelligence` to decide whether a relevant topic deserves content.
Use `personal-brand-writer` only after the thesis is clear.
