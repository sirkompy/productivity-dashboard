# Personal Brand v1

## Positioning

**B2B Growth & GTM at the intersection of Business Development, Sales, AI and Financial Infrastructure.**

The brand should signal an operator who understands how technical B2B products get distribution, not a generic marketer, crypto influencer, or AI-news account.

## Core pillars

1. **B2B Sales / Business Development / GTM**
   - ICP and buying situations
   - outbound and pipeline
   - partnerships and distribution
   - enterprise sales
   - market entry
   - positioning and messaging

2. **AI & Automation**
   - AI for sales, research, marketing and productivity
   - agents and practical workflows
   - automation design
   - lessons from building internal systems

3. **Fintech / Stablecoins / Financial Infrastructure**
   - payments and money movement
   - stablecoin infrastructure
   - wallets and rails
   - privacy, compliance and institutional adoption
   - infrastructure GTM

4. **Marketing / Distribution**
   - product marketing
   - content and demand generation
   - category and positioning
   - channel strategy

## Ecosystem context

Stellar, Solana and other blockchain ecosystems are **context tags**, not independent content pillars.

Use them only when they strengthen a core topic, for example:
- stablecoin distribution on Stellar
- GTM for infrastructure companies in Solana
- ecosystem partnerships as a BD motion
- payments adoption across different rails

## Voice

**Operator, not guru. Smart, clear, practical, slightly opinionated, evidence-driven.**

Target profile:
- expertise / authority: 4/5
- practicality: 5/5
- technical depth: 3/5
- provocativeness: 3/5
- personal tone: 3/5
- humor: 2/5
- corporate polish: 2/5
- hype: 1/5

## Writing rules

Prefer:
- concrete observations over generic advice
- first-hand lessons when genuinely available
- specific examples and data
- clear causal uncertainty
- one strong idea per post
- useful frameworks only when they simplify reality
- short, natural sentences

Avoid:
- invented personal stories
- fake certainty
- generic motivational conclusions
- excessive emojis
- empty contrarian hooks
- crypto hype
- corporate PR language
- broetry
- stock AI patterns such as "It's not X, it's Y"
- "7 lessons that changed everything" unless the source material genuinely supports it

## Content test

Before drafting, answer:
1. Is this inside the brand territory?
2. Is there a real insight, evidence, experience or useful synthesis?
3. Can the author add something beyond summarizing the source?
4. Would this still be worth publishing without algorithmic incentives?

If the answer to 2 or 3 is no, do not create a post.
